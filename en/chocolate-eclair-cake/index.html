Chocolate Eclair Cake Dessert - Quick Recipes

Chocolate Éclair Cake layers graham crackers, vanilla pudding, whipped topping, and chocolate frosting into a nostalgic, no-bake dessert that chills into sliceable bliss. Think of this as a shortcut mille-feuille with Midwest charm. The graham crackers soften into “pastry,” the vanilla filling sets creamy without cooking, and the chocolate lid ties it all together.
