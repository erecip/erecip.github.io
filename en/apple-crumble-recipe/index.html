Apple Crumble Recipe - Quick Recipes

A comforting dessert classic, apple crumble is beloved for its contrast of tender, syrupy fruit and golden, crumbly topping. This recipe features Granny Smith apples and a cinnamon-scented oat crust. The topping bakes into a crisp, buttery layer, while the apple filling becomes soft and jammy. Serve it warm with a scoop of vanilla ice
