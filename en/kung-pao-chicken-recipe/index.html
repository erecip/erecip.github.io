Kung Pao Chicken Recipe - Quick Recipes

A stir-fry classic, Kung Pao Chicken is a flavorful Chinese dish known for its irresistible sweet, savory, and spicy sauce with a signature numbing heat from Sichuan peppercorns. This takeout favorite brings the perfect balance of crunch, spice, and rich umami notes, made in under 30 minutes using tender chicken, roasted peanuts, and dried chilies.
