shrimp ceviche - Quick Recipes

A vibrant shrimp ceviche is a must-have for any gathering or as a refreshing treat on warm days. Packed with lime-marinated shrimp, zesty jalapeño, and creamy avocado, this gluten-free dish is perfect for dipping, layering, or stuffing into tacos. This versatile appetizer shines whether made with raw shrimp "cooked" in lime juice or pre-cooked shrimp
