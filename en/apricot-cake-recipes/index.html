Apricot Cake Recipes - Quick Recipes

This apricot cake (made with fresh apricots) is incredibly moist and fruit-forward, showcasing the sweet-tart flavor of ripe summer apricots in every bite. It’s tender, golden, and elegantly topped with halved apricots and almonds. Blending pureed apricots into the batter deepens the flavor, while whole fruit on top gives a juicy burst in every slice.
